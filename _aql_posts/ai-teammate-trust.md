I conducted a study on how helping behaviour and human-like cues shape trust in human–AI teams. The research used a simulated drone-control task to explore how people responded to an AI teammate under different conditions.

I was interested in what makes an AI teammate feel worth relying on, and whether helpful behaviour and human-like cues matter in combination.
