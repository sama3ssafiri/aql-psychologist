About the anatomy

Real cortical folds from the FreeSurfer fsaverage5 template, with Desikan–Killiany atlas outlines. Cerebellum and caudate surfaces use the FS86 MNI atlas distributed by brainmontageplot. This is a composite educational view. Outlines show approximate anatomical extents, not the studies’ measured activation clusters. The hypothalamus is shown as an approximate location. Remembering rejection

A meta-analysis found these regions were consistently recruited in studies of reliving rejection by a significant other.

During spousal hand-holding under threat, higher marital quality was associated with lower threat-related activity in the regions below.

Attachment anxiety & exclusion

In a simulated social-exclusion task, greater attachment anxiety was associated with stronger responses in the anterior insula and dorsal anterior cingulate. These broad outlines illustrate the related anatomy.

DeWall et al. (2012)

Remembering jealousy

A study of women who had experienced infidelity reported insula and anterior cingulate involvement when listening to descriptions of their own jealousy experience. The right-sided outlines are anatomical illustrations.
