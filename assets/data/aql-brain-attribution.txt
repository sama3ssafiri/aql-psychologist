Cortical geometry: FreeSurfer fsaverage5 pial_left.gii.gz and pial_right.gii.gz,
redistributed by Nilearn. Surface reconstructed from MRI-derived average anatomy.
https://github.com/nilearn/nilearn/tree/main/nilearn/datasets/data/fsaverage5
https://surfer.nmr.mgh.harvard.edu/fswiki/FreeSurferSoftwareLicense

Cortical labels: Desikan–Killiany aparc annotations on fsaverage5, redistributed
by brainmontageplot. Right anterior insula uses the anterior (y > 0) part of the
right insula parcel; anterior cingulate combines rostral and caudal anterior
cingulate parcels. The left orbitofrontal outline uses the lateral orbitofrontal
parcel. Superior frontal outline uses the right superior frontal parcel as an
anatomical illustration, not a claim of study-specific lateralisation or extent.
These broad anatomical parcels are not the original studies' activation clusters.

Cerebellum and right caudate: FS86 subcortical volume fs86_subcortical.nii.gz,
labels 1/10 and 12 respectively, distributed by brainmontageplot in MNI space.
https://github.com/kjamison/brainmontageplot/tree/main/brainmontage/atlases

Cortical and volumetric templates are composed for an educational display;
this is not a registered multimodal atlas suitable for measurements. The
hypothalamus is an explicitly approximate location indicator, not a segmentation.
No cerebellar activity is implied by its inclusion in the background anatomy.

Fischl et al. (1999), High-resolution intersubject averaging and a coordinate
system for the cortical surface. Human Brain Mapping 8(4):272–284.
Desikan et al. (2006), An automated labeling system for subdividing the human
cerebral cortex on MRI scans into gyral based regions of interest.
NeuroImage 31(3):968–980.
