Custom activities and practical tools for use in sessions.

## [Charge](/resources/charge/)

A short wireframe game: press for a small pulse, or wait for a bigger bloom. Explore the urge to act, then reflect on what helped you pause.

[Try Charge →](/resources/charge/)


## [The Bigger Picture](/resources/bigger-picture/)

A puzzle about tolerating frustration, taking a step back and finding a different fit. The final piece changes once; the whole picture still has a solution.

[Try the puzzle →](/resources/bigger-picture/)
