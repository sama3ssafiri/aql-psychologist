Imagine a friend offers to help with something important. One question is whether they usually follow through. Another is whether you feel able to ask for clarity, set a limit or speak up if plans change.

Those questions can lead to different answers. You might feel confident in your own judgement and still have good reason to hesitate. You might also have plenty of evidence that someone is reliable and still find it hard to lean on them.

What would help you decide how much to rely on them this time?
