About the anatomy

Real cortical folds from the FreeSurfer fsaverage5 template, with Desikan–Killiany atlas outlines. Cerebellum and caudate surfaces use the FS86 MNI atlas distributed by brainmontageplot. This is a composite educational view. Outlines show approximate anatomical extents, not the studies’ measured activation clusters. The hypothalamus is shown as an approximate location.
